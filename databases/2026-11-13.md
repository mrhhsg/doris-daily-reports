为下推的局部聚合基数估计增加上限，同时受其输入估计与当前分组列取值域大小乘积约束，且不会调高估计，减少基数高估导致的连接构建侧误选。

重要性与适用范围：适用于局部聚合下推后，上层连接估计超过下层聚合输入规模的场景。更合理的估计有助于避免构建过大的连接侧，并改善运行时过滤机会，降低连接执行开销。

Partial aggregate pushdown gives the new lower aggregate the estimate of the aggregate above the join. That estimate is often the join estimate itself, and it can exceed the lower aggregate's own input. The build side optimizer then sees a huge aggregate and builds the other side of the join instead.

The lower aggregate's estimate is now capped at both its input estimate and the product of the current domains of its groups. The cap never raises an estimate.

At SF1000 the only plan change is the build side of one join in TPC-DS q74. The q10 case shows up with filter selectivity sampling (\#28664), whose estimates make the original estimate larger than the input.

针对聚合状态使用 arena 且无法溢写的哈希聚合，调整放弃当前哈希表、转入外部执行及扩容的判断，避免 arena 内存反复触发放弃并重建重复分组状态，减少无效处理和内存增长。

重要性与适用范围：适用于 quantile\_cont、arg\_max、approx\_top\_k 等无法溢写状态的聚合，尤其是 arena 超过线程内存限制的场景；可避免反复重建状态加剧内存压力，有溢写方案或不使用 arena 的布局保持原路径。

官方原文证据（保留基准及适用条件）：
Some aggregates keep their states in the arena but have no spill plan (e.g. \`quantile\_cont\`, \`arg\_max\`, the \`top-N\` in h2o q08, \`approx\_top\_k\` etc). Their memory can't shrink when the sink abandons, because abandoning clears only the pointer table and the arena stays. Once the arena alone is above the thread limit, Sink abandons on every chunk. Each abandon re-creates the states of every repeated group in the arena, so memory only grows. Going external doesn't help either, because it can't release the arena.

This PR changes three heuristics, only for layouts without a spill plan:
- Abandon pressure: with a non-empty arena, abandon again only once the HT's spillable part (everything but the arena) has grown by \`thread\_limit / AGGREGATE\_STATE\_SPILL\_DIVISOR\` since the last abandon, so re-created states don't trigger the next abandon.
- Going external: the decision ignores the arena, which stays in memory either way.
- TryGrow: the arena doesn't count against the limit, and the cost of a duplicate row includes its arena bytes.

Layouts with a spill plan or no arena take the old paths.
