PR #28631｜主线 / main

将缓冲区管理集合中追加到空子链、长度超过标准向量大小的定长类型列表子数据，存为单个 LARGE_FLAT 条目，使重复扫描可直接读取而无需重新复制子数据。

重要性与适用范围：适用于反复扫描大型列表的物化结果，例如交叉连接的物化侧及多个消费者共享的物化 CTE；可避免每次扫描都完整复制列表子数据，优化范围限于符合新表示条件的子数据。

官方原文证据（保留基准及适用条件）：
First step of the split requested in #27991. That PR shares large list children between collections and extends them in place, and will be rebased onto this one and measured against it.

Problem

ColumnDataCollection stores a list child longer than STANDARD_VECTOR_SIZE as a chain of vector-sized entries. ReadVectorInternal only reads without copying when the chain has a single entry, so every scan of a collection holding large lists re-materializes the children. Operators that rescan a collection pay a full child copy per scan, for example the materialized side of a cross product (one scan per input chunk of the other side) or a materialized CTE with several consumers.

Implementation

In buffer-managed collections, a child of a constant-size type that is appended into an empty child chain with more than STANDARD_VECTOR_SIZE entries is now stored as one entry of a new representation, ColumnDataRepresentation::LARGE_FLAT, in the collection's own blocks. Ownership is unchanged: scans hand out the same kind of view into a pinned block as any zero-copy scan today.
