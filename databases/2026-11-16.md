[PR \#131569｜主线 / master](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/131569)

优化 Compact 数据分片的多阶段 PREWHERE 读取路径：只要前序步骤完全过滤掉某个粒度，后续步骤和最终读取便跳过该粒度，不再受读取块大小影响。

重要性与适用范围：适用于 Compact 分片上能由前序 PREWHERE 步骤完全排除部分粒度的查询，可减少后续列读取中的重复工作；Wide 分片仍保留原有处理策略，不受此次优化影响。

官方原文证据（保留基准及适用条件）：
\#\#\# Changelog entry (a \[user-readable short description\](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/blob/master/docs/changelog\_entry\_guidelines.md) of the changes that goes into CHANGELOG.md):
A multi-step \`PREWHERE\` over Compact parts no longer reads the granules that an earlier step filtered out completely again in its later steps and in the final read, whatever the read block size. For the query from \#131382 this reads 45% fewer compressed bytes and runs up to 1.5x faster. Closes \#131382.

\#\#\# Description

On Compact parts, a granule that an earlier \`PREWHERE\` step filtered out completely was read again by every later step and by the final read, unless the emptied granules were more than half of the rows of one read. How many granules one read covers is chosen by the block size predictor, so \`query\_plan\_remove\_unused\_columns = 1\` (it narrows the \`PREWHERE\` output, and reads grow from 1 to 4 granules) made the query in \#131382 read every primary-key-selected row in every step.
