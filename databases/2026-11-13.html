官方原文证据（保留基准及适用条件）：
Partial aggregate pushdown gives the new lower aggregate the estimate of the aggregate above the join. That estimate is often the join estimate itself, and it can exceed the lower aggregate's own input. The build side optimizer then sees a huge aggregate and builds the other side of the join instead.

Example, TPC-H q10 at SF3000:

  • The lower GROUP BY o_custkey has 63M groups but was estimated at 1385M rows.

The lower aggregate's estimate is now capped at both its input estimate and the product of the current domains of its groups. The cap never raises an estimate.

At SF1000 the only plan change is the build side of one join in TPC-DS q74. The q10 case shows up with filter selectivity sampling (#28664), whose estimates make the original estimate larger than the input.

重要性与适用范围：适用于 quantile_cont、arg_max、approx_top_k 等无法溢写状态的聚合，尤其是 arena 超过线程内存限制的场景；可避免反复重建状态加剧内存压力，有溢写方案或不使用 arena 的布局保持原路径。

官方原文证据（保留基准及适用条件）：
Some aggregates keep their states in the arena but have no spill plan (e.g. quantile_cont, arg_max, the top-N in h2o q08, approx_top_k etc). Their memory can't shrink when the sink abandons, because abandoning clears only the pointer table and the arena stays. Once the arena alone is above the thread limit, Sink abandons on every chunk. Each abandon re-creates the states of every repeated group in the arena, so memory only grows. Going external doesn't help either, because it can't release the arena.

This PR changes three heuristics, only for layouts without a spill plan:

  • Abandon pressure: with a non-empty arena, abandon again only once the HT's spillable part (everything but the arena) has grown by thread_limit / AGGREGATE_STATE_SPILL_DIVISOR since the last abandon, so re-created states don't trigger the next abandon.

Layouts with a spill plan or no arena take the old paths.

当连接器扫描的工作与输出均独立时，将活跃输入检查限定到当前驱动，使已完成扫描的驱动不再等待其他驱动的读取器；共享输出或共享任务队列等场景仍保留全局检查。

重要性与适用范围：优化分组执行中扫描任务负载不均时的调度：避免已完成驱动继续占用执行槽、延迟后续逻辑驱动。适用范围限于独立扫描驱动，保留暂停读取器等生命周期保护，未提供延迟收益数据。

官方原文证据（保留基准及适用条件）：

Why I'm doing:

An independent connector scan driver can finish all of its work but remain unfinished while another driver has an active reader. Under Group Execution, the completed driver then retains an execution slot and delays the next logical driver. This becomes costly when grouped range scans have uneven workloads.

Related:.

What I'm doing:

Scope the existing active-input check to the current driver only when both work and output are independent. Shared output, shared morsel queues, random split redistribution, or missing queue-factory metadata retain the factory-wide check. Own registrations still protect paused readers and source/split publication; status, I/O, future work, queued tasks, buffered final chunks, and notifications keep their existing lifecycle.
